Business first, then the system

I didn't start in ERP. I started as a Operations Specialist at a OLA Foods startup, then wrote code as a full-stack developer intern, then ran social media campaigns. That path means I understand the business problem before I think about the system solution.

Most ERP consultants speak system. I speak business first, because I've sat on the other side of the table.

I know what it looks like when inventory doesn't match the books and the finance team is asking questions the system can't answer. That context makes me a better consultant.

Today I work on Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance and Operations (SCM) and Business Central, helping manufacturing, trading and distribution businesses treat ERP as an operational change, not only a software deployment.
